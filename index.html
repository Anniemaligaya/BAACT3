Sustainable Lipa City Project

Earn Rewards by Recycling in Local Malls

The EcoCoins project introduces automated reward kiosks inside major malls across Lipa City. Turn your plastic containers and aluminum cans into digital EcoPoints that unlock store incentives.

Create Free Account Learn More
Project Summary
  • Location: Selected Malls in Lipa City
  • Accepted Items: Plastic Bottles, Aluminum Cans
  • Incentive: EcoPoints converted into store perks
  • Platform: Web-based account tracking system

Core Objectives

Key goals driving the implementation of the EcoCoins reward system.

Community Disposal Support

Provides accessible drop-off points within commercial establishments, motivating shoppers to separate recyclables from regular waste.

Point Incentivization

Rewards citizens for environmental participation by distributing digital EcoPoints according to deposit volume and weight.

Digital Balance Ledger

Enables users to conveniently verify accumulated balances, view historical recycling records, and browse available merchant vouchers.

User Participation Guide

How mall patrons interact with the web platform and physical kiosks.

1. Clean & Sort Recyclables
Phase 1

Gather empty plastic beverage bottles, soda cans, and clean disposable packaging materials.

2. Drop Off at Mall Kiosks
Phase 2

Visit designated EcoCoins deposit units located inside participating malls in Lipa City.

3. Credit Your Points
Phase 3

Authenticate your account at the kiosk terminal to instantly record new EcoPoints to your profile.

4. Claim Mall Incentives
Phase 4

Redeem accumulated points online for merchant coupons, parking discounts, and retail perks.
